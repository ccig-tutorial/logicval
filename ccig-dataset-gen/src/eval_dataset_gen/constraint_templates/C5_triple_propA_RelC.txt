% C5 Conditional (Subgraph): Every triple (X1,X2, X3) with X1 having (P1'=V1'), X2 having (P2'=V2'), X3 (P3', V3') must be related by D1'(X1, X2) and D2'(X2, X3).
:- object(X1), object(X2), object(X3), X1!=X2, X2!=X3, X1!=X3,
   hasProperty(X1, P1', V1'), hasProperty(X2, P2', V2'), hasProperty(X3, P3', V3'),
   V1'!=V2', V2'!=V3', V1'!=V3',
   #count{ 1 : hasRelationship(X1, X2, D1') ; 2 : hasRelationship(X2, X3, D2') } != 2.
