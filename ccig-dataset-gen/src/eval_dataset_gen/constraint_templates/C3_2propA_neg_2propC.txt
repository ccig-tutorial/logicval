% C3 Conditional Object, negated literal: every object with P1'=V1' and P2'!=V2' must have P3'=V3' and P4'=V4'. 
:- object(X), hasProperty(X, P1', V1'), not hasProperty(X, P2', V2'), P1'!=P2', P1'!=P3', P2'!=P3', P1'!=P4', P2'!=P4', P3'!=P4', #count{ 1 : hasProperty(X, P3', V3') ; 2 : hasProperty(X, P4', V4') } != 2.

:- #count {X: object(X), hasProperty(X, P1', V1'), not hasProperty(X, P2', V2')} = 0.
