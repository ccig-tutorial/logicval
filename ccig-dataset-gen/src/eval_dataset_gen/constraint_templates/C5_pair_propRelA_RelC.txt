% C5 Conditional (Subgraph): Every pair (X1,X2) with X1 having (P1'=V1'), X2 having (P2'=V2'), related by D1', must also be related by D2'.
:- object(X1), object(X2), X1 != X2, hasProperty(X1, P1', V1'), hasProperty(X2, P2', V2'), V1'!=V2', hasRelationship(X1, X2, D1'), not hasRelationship(X1, X2, D2').

:- #count {X1, X2: object(X1), object(X2), X1 != X2, hasProperty(X1, P1', V1'), hasProperty(X2, P2', V2'), hasRelationship(X1, X2, D1'), V1'!=V2'} = 0.
